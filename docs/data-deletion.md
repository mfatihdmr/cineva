# Cineva — Data Deletion

**Contact:** muhammetfatihdemr@gmail.com

## How to delete your data

Cineva stores all user data locally on your device. There are three ways to
delete it:

### 1. In-app (recommended)

Open **Settings → Data → Delete all data**. This immediately and permanently
wipes every user record: lists, ratings, notes, recent searches, followed
people and the TMDB cache.

### 2. Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Cineva removes all locally stored data.

### 3. By e-mail

If you cannot access the app, e-mail **muhammetfatihdemr@gmail.com**
from the address associated with your request and we will confirm deletion.

## What data exists

- Lists (watchlist, watching, watched, favorites), ratings, notes
- Recent searches (max 20) and followed people
- Preferences (theme, language, region, adult-content switch)
- TMDB response cache

All of the above stays **on your device only** — it is never sent to a Cineva
server, so deleting it locally deletes it everywhere.

## Third-party data

- **TMDB**: anonymous API requests only; TMDB keeps server logs under its own
  privacy policy. If you signed in with a TMDB account, delete that account
  at themoviedb.org.
- **Google AdMob**: advertising identifier and ad interactions. Reset your ad
  ID via Android Settings → Google → Ads, or use the in-app ad privacy options
  (Settings → Ads → Ad privacy options).
