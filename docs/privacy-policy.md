# Cineva — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026
**Contact:** muhammetfatihdemr@gmail.com
**App:** Cineva (`app.cineva.cineva`), version 1.0.0

## 1. Overview

Cineva is a movie and TV discovery app. It has **no server of its own**. It does
not collect personal data, does not require an account, and never sends your
information to a Cineva server.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

The following data stays on your device in local storage and is deleted when
you uninstall the app or use the in-app "delete all data" option:

- Your lists (watchlist, watching, watched, favorites)
- Your ratings and notes
- Recent searches (max 20 entries) and people you follow
- Preferences: theme, UI language, content language, region, adult-content switch
- A TMDB response cache for offline browsing (clearable in Settings > Data)

## 3. What is sent to TMDB

Anonymous API requests go to themoviedb.org to render content. The API key
belongs to the app developer and is not linked to your identity.

Requests carry only your content language and region parameters. No name,
e-mail, phone number, or contacts are sent. TMDB keeps server logs under its
own privacy policy: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The SDK may use:

- A cookie-like advertising identifier
- Approximate location (country code)
- Device and model information

These are governed by Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can:

- Disable personalized ads in **Settings > Ads**
- Open ad privacy options (UMP consent) in the app once ads are loaded
- Reset your ad identity via Android Settings > Google > Ads

## 5. Permissions

The app only needs **internet access**. It never asks for location, camera,
contacts, microphone, or storage permissions.

## 6. Children's privacy

The app targets ages 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. No
behavioural advertising is served to children.

## 7. Third-party services

- **TMDB** — content data; see https://www.themoviedb.org/terms-of-use
- **Google AdMob** — advertising; see https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **YouTube** — trailers open in official YouTube sources

## 8. Data deletion and contact

Because data stays on the device, uninstalling removes it all. For questions,
data requests, or complaints: **muhammetfatihdemr@gmail.com**

## 9. Changes

Changes to this policy will be announced in release notes and the in-app legal
screen.
